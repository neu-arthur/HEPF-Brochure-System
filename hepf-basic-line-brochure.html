The Idea

Standard by design.
That's exactly the point.

Most modular space starts with a blank page: drawings, decisions, revisions, lead time. Basic Line starts with five layouts that are already engineered, already proven and already in production. You choose the unit and the quantity — we handle the rest.

01

Shorter lead times

Standard configurations mean there is no design phase to work through. Units move from order to site in a fraction of the time a bespoke build takes.

02

Lower unit cost

Repeatable production and a fixed specification remove the cost of one-off engineering — so the budget goes into the number of units, not the drawings.

03

Certified build quality

Manufactured to European Union standards under ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015. Galvanised steel frame, powder-coated cladding, factory-fitted services.
